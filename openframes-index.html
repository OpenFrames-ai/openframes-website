The problem

Health evidence keeps moving

New trials appear, studies get retracted, and a claim that held last year may not hold today. Yet evidence is assessed once and left to age.

AI agents answer health questions at massive scale, and they get the evidence wrong, even with web search:

  • Hallucinated citations to studies that don't exist
  • Confident answers when the evidence is thin
  • Hardly ever a "we don't know"
The solution

Grounding that shows its work

OpenFrames is the evidence layer for health AI. It finds the studies behind every claim, grades them and keeps them current.

RAG

One search, then a black box

  • One semantic search per question
  • No record of what was retrieved
  • No way to tell if what came back was useful
  • Nothing kept for the next AI query
OpenFrames

Grounding you can audit

  • An exact record of every study retrieved
  • Which evidence the agent used for which part of its answer
  • Scores and analysis for every study against the claim
  • Kept, dated and updated when research changes
The core object

A Frame

OpenFrames builds a shareable, reusable object for every health claim: the studies for and against it, graded, with an advisory status and a dated history.

Every Frame lives at a stable URL. Agents cite it, reviewers attach it to dossiers, and one update reaches everyone who relies on it.
Example
ClaimFish oil cuts heart attack risk
TrialsTwo large omega-3 trials, 38,949 adults: no clear drop in heart attacks or strokes
Cohort415,737 UK adults: regular use linked to more atrial fibrillation in healthy people
StatusInsufficient evidence (advisory)
UpdatedMay 2024, a new cohort study challenges the claim
URLframes.sh/e/7f3a9c

Substantiation specialists

Consultancies that build claim dossiers for supplement and health brands spend weeks finding, reading and grading studies, and a dossier starts aging the day it ships. OpenFrames gives your team a graded starting point for every claim and a dated record of how the evidence was assessed. It is designed around the FTC standard of competent and reliable scientific evidence.

  • Start each dossier from a graded evidence set
  • Check a client's claims against the current literature
  • Keep a dated record for every claim you sign off
  • Get flagged when new research affects a client's claim
